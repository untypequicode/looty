---
title: "Moodle"
description: "Browse your Moodle courses, grades and files from Looty."
---

If your school uses Moodle, link your account once and use it from Looty.

- **Automatic sign-in**: open the Moodle site already signed in, without typing your credentials again.
- **Moodle dashboard**: panels (deadlines, courses, folder, pinned course…) that you place and resize freely on a grid. It is not a task board.
- **Courses**: favorite (star) and hidden courses work the same in Looty and on the Moodle site. Create your own **course folders** in Looty to group courses; they do not exist on Moodle. Moodle **categories** stay as the school defines them.
- **Personalisation**: give a course a short name, a color and an icon, for you only.
- **Grades**: Looty reads your Moodle gradebook (course grade, items, scale, teacher comment) without being able to change it.
- **Offline**: keep course files on the device.
