---
title: "Sharing"
description: "Share a page with people inside or outside the space: levels, scope, groups, outsiders and access requests."
---

Open a page and press **Share**. Anyone who can **manage** the page can use the dialog, not only space administrators.

## Who has access

The dialog starts with a summary, for example "12 people have access, 3 outside the space", then lists everyone with:

- their **level**: *Can view*, *Can comment*, *Can edit*, *Can manage*, or *No access* (a refusal that beats the space role);
- the **scope**: *this page only* or *this page and sub-pages*. The closest share wins: a share or a refusal on a sub-page overrides what comes from above;
- the **origin** of the right: added on this page, from a parent page (named only if you can see it), through a group, or through the person's role in the space.

Changing the level of an inherited row, or of a member who follows their role, sets a direct share on this page. Removing a direct share goes back to the role, the parent page or the group. Changes apply immediately, including to open sessions.

## Adding people and groups

Type a name or email. You find the space members, the accounts of the instance and the groups. Giving **manage** asks for confirmation: that person can share the page with anyone and delete it with its sub-pages.

An instance administrator can limit who can be found (Settings, Sharing): everyone, people who share a space with you, or only a full email address. Groups of accounts are created there too; sharing with a group gives each member the access and follows the group when people join or leave. A direct share to a person always beats a group share, and a refusal from any group wins.

## People outside the space

A person without a role in the space who receives a share is a **guest**: they see only the shared page and its sub-pages (never the parents), in *Shared with me*. They are marked *Outside the space* in the dialog, and can be mentioned in the page and its comments once they can read it. Sharing sends an in-app notification, and an email the first time for a guest (see the notification settings).

## Request access

Opening the link of a page you cannot see offers **Request access**. The people who manage the page get a notification with *Approve* and *Decline*, also available in the Share dialog. Approving gives *Can view* on the page and its sub-pages. The page itself is never named to the requester, and a declined request can be repeated the next day.

## Shared with me

Pages other people shared with you in spaces you are not a member of are grouped by space, most recent first, with a search. Each shows your level, who shared it (or the group) and a path limited to the parents you can see, with "…" when others are hidden. The list refreshes by itself.

## Moving a page

Moving a page under another parent lists who would gain or lose access and asks you to confirm. Moving it to another space removes its individual shares.
