---
title: "Announcements and polls"
description: "Tell members something and ask them a question."
---

An **announcement** passes information to all or part of a workspace: a room change, an exam date… Authors need the *Publish announcements* right.

- Recipients are linked groups and chosen people; they are notified and each sees it as read or unread.
- An announcement can be pinned and can expire.
- A **poll** is a question with options carried by an announcement: single or multiple choice, change or withdraw your vote while it is open. It can be anonymous (only totals visible), show results only after voting, and close at a date or by hand.
