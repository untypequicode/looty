---
title: "Groups"
description: "Name sets of members to mention or assign them together."
---

A **group** is a named set of members of a workspace. It carries no rights: it only saves you from picking people one by one.

- Mention a group to notify all its members.
- Assign a group to a task or an event, or address an announcement to it.
- A person can belong to several groups.

A group added to a task, event or announcement is **linked**: when someone joins the group they are assigned (or see the announcement); when someone leaves, they are removed, unless they were also assigned individually or through another linked group. Finished tasks do not change, and deleting a group leaves the people in place.
