---
title: "Personal items"
description: "Documents, boards and tasks that only you can see, even in a shared workspace."
---

A **personal item** is a document, project, Drive file, board or task of a shared workspace that only its owner sees, not even the administrators: your lab work under the course page, your notebook in the group project. What you store inside stays personal too.

- Only the person who creates an item can make it personal, and only its owner can share it again.
- If you leave the workspace, your personal items move to your personal space, under the name of the former workspace.
- Do not confuse it with your **personal space**, which is a private space of its own.
