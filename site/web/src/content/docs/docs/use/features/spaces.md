---
title: "Workspaces and roles"
description: "Workspaces, members, roles and invitations in Looty."
---

A **workspace** gathers the members, boards, documents and calendar of one team, class or project. You belong to as many workspaces as you are invited to, and you also have a **personal space** that only you see.

- **Roles** decide what each member can do (read, edit, administer, publish announcements…). Administrators set them in the workspace settings.
- **Invitations**: administrators invite people by link or email; guests can be given limited access.
- **Tabs**: pages you open stay in the tab bar of the workspace, each with its own path, like in a browser.
