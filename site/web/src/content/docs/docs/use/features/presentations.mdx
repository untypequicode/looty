A **presentation** is a page made of slides. Several people edit it at the same time, and it is shared, searched, commented and moved around exactly like a document.

## Create one

In the sidebar, open the **New** menu of the documents and choose **Presentation**. It opens with one blank slide in 16:9. Presentations show a presentation icon in the page tree.

## The screen

- **Slide rail** (left): thumbnails of all slides, with the colored initials of the people who are looking at each one. **New slide** adds one after the current slide; the buttons duplicate, move up, move down and delete it. You can also **drag a thumbnail** to reorder, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> on a focused thumbnail.
- **Canvas** (center): the current slide, scaled to fit. The toolbar above adds a **text box**, a **rectangle**, an **ellipse**, a **line** or an **image** (uploaded as an attachment of the page).
- **Properties** (right): background of the slide, or fill, stroke, text color, font size, alignment, position, size, angle, order, alignment of several elements, grouping, lock, and an **alternative text** for screen readers. Colors come from the same palette as the page editor.

## Working with elements

- Click to select, <kbd>Shift</kbd> + click or drag a rectangle on the empty slide to select several. Drag to move: **guides** snap to the slide and to the other elements (hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to move freely).
- Drag a handle to resize (<kbd>Shift</kbd> keeps the proportions, <kbd>Alt</kbd> resizes from the center); drag the round handle above the element to rotate (<kbd>Shift</kbd> rotates by 15 degrees).
- Double-click a text box (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>) to type in it, with bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and lists. <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves the text.
- Everything you change is visible to the others as you do it; their selections are outlined in their color, with their name, and their pointer is shown on the slide.

## Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo, redo (your own changes only) |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Delete the selection |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd> | Duplicate |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd> / <kbd>X</kbd> / <kbd>V</kbd> | Copy, cut, paste (between slides of the same presentation) |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>A</kbd> | Select all elements of the slide |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd> | Group, ungroup |
| Arrows, <kbd>Shift</kbd> + arrows | Move by 1 or 10 units |
| <kbd>Page up</kbd> / <kbd>Page down</kbd>, <kbd>Home</kbd>, <kbd>End</kbd> | Change slide |

## Present

**Present** (above the canvas) starts the slide show from the current slide, full screen when the browser allows it.

| Key or gesture | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>→</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> <kbd>Page down</kbd>, click, swipe left | Next step or slide (a clicker works the same way) |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>Page up</kbd>, swipe right | Previous |
| <kbd>Home</kbd> / <kbd>End</kbd> | First / last slide |
| A number then <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Go to that slide |
| <kbd>G</kbd> | Overview of all slides |
| <kbd>B</kbd> or <kbd>.</kbd> / <kbd>W</kbd> or <kbd>,</kbd> | Black / white screen (press again to come back) |
| <kbd>L</kbd> | Laser pointer |
| <kbd>+</kbd> <kbd>-</kbd> <kbd>0</kbd> | Zoom in, out, reset (the zoom follows the pointer) |
| <kbd>F</kbd> / <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Full screen / leave |

The control bar at the bottom hides when you stop moving the pointer and comes back with it or with the keyboard. The slide number is announced to screen readers at each change, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> always leaves. Only the slide being shown is rendered, so long presentations stay light.

### Transitions and animations

In the properties panel of a slide, **Transition** picks none, fade, slide or zoom for that slide, and **Default transition** sets the one used by the others. **Animations** lists, in playing order, the elements that enter step by step: select an element and add an animation (appear, fade in, move in), then choose whether it starts **on click** or **after the previous** one, and reorder them. Animations and transitions are skipped when your system asks to reduce motion.

### Speaker notes and presenter view

Write the **speaker notes** of each slide in the properties panel; they are never shown on the slide. **Presenter view** opens a second window, meant for your own screen, with the current and next slide, the notes (with larger or smaller text), a timer, the clock and a progress bar. Both windows stay in sync in both directions and use the same browser, so you can move on from either one.

### Let others follow you

While you present, the people who have the presentation open see **"Someone is presenting"** (with their name) and can **Follow**. They see your slide and step as you move. If they go back or ahead on their own, they stay free until they choose **Back to presenter**, and **Stop following** leaves the live view. The **share** button of the control bar turns this off if you want to present privately. Nothing more than the page rights is needed: the live state travels through the collaboration channel of the presentation.

### Public link, loop and print

A presentation shared by public link opens a read-only viewer without any account, with a **Start the slide show** button. Add `?kiosk=8` to the link (or tick **Loop automatically**) to loop on its own, 8 seconds per slide, for a screen at an event. Signed-in people can also open `/w/<workspace>/present/<page>` for the slide show alone, with `?mode=presenter` for the presenter view. **Print** (or the browser print command) prints all slides, one per page, to paper or PDF.

## Rights

Rights are those of the page: people who can only read see the slides but cannot change anything, and a presentation shared by link follows the page sharing. On a phone the presentation is meant for reading; editing works best on a tablet or a computer.

## Limits

A presentation holds up to 300 slides and 500 elements per slide. Themes and import or export (PowerPoint, PDF) are planned and not available yet.
