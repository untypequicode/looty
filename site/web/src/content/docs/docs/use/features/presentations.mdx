title: "Presentations"
description: "Build slide decks together in real time: slides, text boxes, shapes and images, with the same sharing as any page."

A **presentation** is a page made of slides. Several people edit it at the same time, and it is shared, searched, commented and moved around exactly like a document.

## Create one

In the sidebar, open the **New** menu of the documents and choose **Presentation**. It opens with one blank slide in 16:9. Presentations show a presentation icon in the page tree.

## The screen

- **Slide rail** (left): thumbnails of all slides, with the colored initials of the people who are looking at each one. **New slide** adds one after the current slide; the buttons duplicate, move up, move down and delete it. You can also **drag a thumbnail** to reorder, or press <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> on a focused thumbnail.
- **Canvas** (center): the current slide, scaled to fit. The toolbar above adds a **text box**, a **rectangle**, an **ellipse**, a **line** or an **image** (uploaded as an attachment of the page).
- **Properties** (right): background of the slide, or fill, stroke, text color, font size, alignment, position, size, angle, order, alignment of several elements, grouping, lock, and an **alternative text** for screen readers. Colors come from the same palette as the page editor.

## Working with elements

- Click to select, <kbd>Shift</kbd> + click or drag a rectangle on the empty slide to select several. Drag to move: **guides** snap to the slide and to the other elements (hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to move freely).
- Drag a handle to resize (<kbd>Shift</kbd> keeps the proportions, <kbd>Alt</kbd> resizes from the center); drag the round handle above the element to rotate (<kbd>Shift</kbd> rotates by 15 degrees).
- Double-click a text box (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>) to type in it, with bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and lists. <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves the text.
- Everything you change is visible to the others as you do it; their selections are outlined in their color, with their name, and their pointer is shown on the slide.

## Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo, redo (your own changes only) |
| <kbd>Delete</kbd> | Delete the selection |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>D</kbd> | Duplicate |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>C</kbd> / <kbd>X</kbd> / <kbd>V</kbd> | Copy, cut, paste (between slides of the same presentation) |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>A</kbd> | Select all elements of the slide |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd>, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>G</kbd> | Group, ungroup |
| Arrows, <kbd>Shift</kbd> + arrows | Move by 1 or 10 units |
| <kbd>Page up</kbd> / <kbd>Page down</kbd>, <kbd>Home</kbd>, <kbd>End</kbd> | Change slide |

## View: zoom, grid, sections

- **Zoom**: the buttons above the canvas fit the slide to the window, show it at 100%, zoom in and out; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + wheel zooms too, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>0</kbd> fits again. On a touch screen, pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to move the view.
- **Grid**: the grid button shows a grid and makes elements snap to it while you move or resize them (hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to move freely). The setting is yours only.
- **Sections**: **Add a section** in the rail names a group of slides starting at the current slide. Click a section title to fold or unfold it; the pencil renames it and the cross removes the section (its slides are kept). Sections are shared with everyone.
- **Arrow and icons**: the toolbar adds an **arrow** (with a head at the end, at both ends or none) and any **icon** of the Lucide set that Looty already includes, searched by name.

## Comments

Select an element (or nothing, for the whole slide) and press the **comment** button of the toolbar: the comments panel of the page opens on the right with a chip that says what the comment is about. Write it, mention people with @, reply, resolve a thread, like on any page. Open threads appear as yellow badges on the thumbnails and on the elements; clicking a thread in the panel takes you to its slide and selects the element. If an element is deleted, its threads stay in the panel marked as about a deleted element. People who can only read cannot comment; people with a comment right can, and mentions and notifications only reach people who can read the page.

## Seeing who does what

Besides pointers and selections, you see **who is editing which element**: a tag with the name and "editing" on the element, and the text cursor and selection of that person inside the text box, live, even if you are only looking at it. The rail shows the avatars of the people on each slide.

## Version history

The page menu has **History**. Versions are saved automatically (one per editing session, like pages) and you can **save a named version**. Pick a version to preview it slide by slide, read-only, with a mark on the slides that differ from the current presentation. **Restore** keeps the current state as a version first, then replaces the content of the presentation by the chosen version for everyone; <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> undoes the restoration. **Duplicate** in the page menu copies the whole presentation.

## Rights

Rights are those of the page: people who can only read see the slides but cannot change anything, and a presentation shared by link follows the page sharing. On a phone the presentation is meant for reading; editing works best on a tablet or a computer.

## Limits

A presentation holds up to 300 slides and 500 elements per slide. Themes, presenter mode, import and export (PowerPoint, PDF) are planned and not available yet. The arrow is a shape: it is not glued to other shapes yet.
