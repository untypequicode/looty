- **Collaborative editing** with presence and cursors; offline edits in the apps merge when you reconnect.
- **Rich blocks**: formulas, tables, code, embeds, callouts.
- **Diagrams**: write a description (flowchart, sequence, Gantt…) and the diagram is laid out for you. Apply a **diagram style** with one click, per block or as the **workspace default**.
- **Graph block**: nodes and edges, directed or not, with labels (weights, flow, capacity); highlight a path or a cut.
- **Drawing**: freehand shapes and arrows.
- **LaTeX projects** compile to PDF (the instance must provide a LaTeX compiler).
- **Sharing**: with the people you choose, or with a **public link**, a secret read-only address that needs no account. A public link never shows other files of a project.

## The editing toolbar

In edit mode a toolbar stays at the top of the page while you scroll. It reflects the selection (active buttons, current block style) and every button shows its keyboard shortcut on hover.

- **Undo and redo**, **block style** (text, headings 1 to 3, quote, code), **bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, inline code**.
- **Colors** open a small palette: 11 colors for the text and 11 for the highlight, a *default* / *no highlight* swatch to clear, and your recent colors on top. One click applies; the colors adapt to the light and dark themes and stay readable on the page. The highlight button next to it toggles your last highlight color. **Lists** (bullets, numbers, to-dos) with **indent and outdent**, **alignment**.
- **Link** (search a document or paste an address), **image**, **file**, **LaTeX formula**, **divider** and **table**. Inside a table, a **table actions** button adds rows and columns, merges or splits cells, sets the row height and deletes the table.
- **Insert** lists the other blocks (callouts, toggles, diagrams, graphs, drawings, embeds, date…): the same list as the `/` menu, which stays available together with the keyboard shortcuts and the selection menu.
- The bar adapts to the width of its column: the least useful groups fold first into a **More** menu (colors, history, insert and alignment last), down to a phone where it keeps style, bold, italic and the Insert menu. On a phone the bar sits just above the keyboard.
- Use the arrow keys, Home and End to move between buttons, and Esc to go back to the text.
- **Show or hide**: the page menu (…) has a *Hide the toolbar* entry. The choice is remembered for your account on all your devices. The bar never appears in read-only mode or on public links.

### Customize the toolbar

The sliders button at the end of the bar (or **Settings → Preferences → Formatting**) opens **Customize toolbar**. Everything is saved with your account and follows you on every device; *Reset to default* undoes it all.

- **Style**: the compact Looty bar, or a **Word-style ribbon** with tabs (*Home*, *Insert*, and a *Table* tab that only appears inside a table), labelled groups, bigger buttons, and a collapse button (collapsed, only the tabs show and a click on a tab opens it). Phones always use the compact bar.
- **Position**: top (default) or bottom. At the bottom the bar is docked to the bottom of the window, above the keyboard on a phone, and menus open upward.
- **Size** (compact or comfortable) and **text labels**. Buttons are at least 32 px on desktop and 40 px on touch screens.
- **Tools**: show or hide each group or tool, and reorder the groups with the arrows (or drag them).
- **Animation and typing**, each a switch: *animate the toolbar* (slide and fade; off by default when your system asks for less motion), *shrink while typing*, *translucent while typing* (with the opacity you choose) and *hide while typing*. The bar comes back, fully opaque, as soon as the mouse moves, is over the bar, or the bar has the focus.
