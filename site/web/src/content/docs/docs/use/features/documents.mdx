- **Collaborative editing** with presence and cursors; offline edits in the apps merge when you reconnect.
- **Rich blocks**: formulas, tables, code, embeds, callouts.
- **Diagrams**: write a description (flowchart, sequence, Gantt…) and the diagram is laid out for you. Apply a **diagram style** with one click, per block or as the **workspace default**.
- **Graph block**: nodes and edges, directed or not, with labels (weights, flow, capacity); highlight a path or a cut.
- **Drawing**: freehand shapes and arrows.
- **LaTeX projects** compile to PDF (the instance must provide a LaTeX compiler).
- **Sharing**: with the people you choose, or with a **public link**, a secret read-only address that needs no account. A public link never shows other files of a project.

## The editing toolbar

In edit mode a toolbar stays at the top of the page while you scroll. It reflects the selection (active buttons, current block style) and every button shows its keyboard shortcut on hover.

- **Undo and redo**, **block style** (text, headings 1 to 3, quote, code), **bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, inline code**.
- **Text color** and **highlight**, **lists** (bullets, numbers, to-dos) with **indent and outdent**, **alignment**.
- **Link** (search a document or paste an address), **image**, **file**, **LaTeX formula**, **divider** and **table**. Inside a table, a **table actions** button adds rows and columns, merges or splits cells, sets the row height and deletes the table.
- **Insert** lists the other blocks (callouts, toggles, diagrams, graphs, drawings, embeds, date…): the same list as the `/` menu, which stays available together with the keyboard shortcuts and the selection menu.
- On a narrow screen, the groups that do not fit fold into a **More** menu. On a phone the bar sits just above the keyboard.
- Use the arrow keys, Home and End to move between buttons, and Esc to go back to the text.
- **Show or hide**: the page menu (…) has a *Hide the toolbar* entry. The choice is remembered for your account on all your devices. The bar never appears in read-only mode or on public links.
