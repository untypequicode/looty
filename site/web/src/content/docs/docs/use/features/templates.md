---
title: "Templates"
description: "Start a board or a document from a template, and save your own."
---

A **template** is a ready-made board or document to start from.

- **New board**: pick a template in the creation dialog. **New document**: use **From a template…** in the New menu.
- **Provided templates**: Project tracking and Retrospective (boards), Meeting notes, Course notes and Lab report (documents), in your language.
- **Your own**: workspace administrators save a board (Board settings) or a document (page menu) as a template. Members who can create boards or documents see them.

A board template keeps the columns, the labels and the open tasks (titles, descriptions, priorities, labels, sub-tasks). People, dates and progress are left out. A document template keeps the title, the icon and the content.

Personal items never become templates. Changing or deleting a template does not change what was created from it. Manage them in **Settings > Templates**.
