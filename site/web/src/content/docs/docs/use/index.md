An **instance** is one installation of Looty at an address such as `looty.my-club.org`, with its own accounts and workspaces. Looty does not offer hosting: your school, club or team runs its own instance, and nobody else holds its data.

## Sign in

1. Ask whoever runs your instance for its address.
2. Open it in a browser, or enter it the first time you open the Android or desktop app.
3. Choose the sign-in method shown on the page (usually a single sign-on button) and sign in with the account your organisation gave you.

An app is linked to **one** instance. A link that points to another instance opens in your browser, not in the app. To change instance in the Android app, use the launcher shortcut *Change server*.
