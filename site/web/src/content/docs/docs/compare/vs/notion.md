title: "Looty vs Notion"
description: "Looty compared with Notion: strengths, limits and when to choose which."
---

**Notion**: a hosted all-in-one workspace (notes, databases, wikis) with a large template ecosystem.

## Where Looty is stronger

- Free software you host: your data stays on your server.
- Native Android and desktop apps that work offline.
- Boards, documents, calendar, mail and Moodle in one tool, with real-time editing.

## Where Notion is stronger

- No hosting to set up: Notion just works, while Looty needs a server and someone to run it.
- Far larger ecosystem: templates, integrations, public API and community.
- Flexible databases with many views and formulas are a Notion specialty.

## Choose Looty if… / Notion if…

Choose Looty if you want to own your data and are happy to run a server. Choose Notion if you want zero administration and a huge template library.

See also the [FAQ](/docs/faq/) and [hosting Looty](/docs/host/).
