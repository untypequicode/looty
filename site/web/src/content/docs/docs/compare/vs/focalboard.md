**Focalboard**: an open-source project-management tool (an alternative to Trello, Notion and Asana), originally from Mattermost.

## Where Looty is stronger

- Actively developed, with documents, calendar and mail around the boards.
- Real-time collaborative documents.
- Native apps for Android and desktop.

## Where Focalboard is stronger

- Focalboard is simple and has been used as a personal or Mattermost-integrated board tool.
- Check its repository for its current maintenance status before adopting it.

## Choose Looty if… / Focalboard if…

Choose Looty if you want a maintained, broader workspace. Choose Focalboard if you want a minimal board and already use Mattermost.

See also the [FAQ](/docs/faq/) and [hosting Looty](/docs/host/).
