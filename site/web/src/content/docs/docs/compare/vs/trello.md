**Trello**: a hosted kanban tool by Atlassian, simple and widely known.

## Where Looty is stronger

- Self-hosted free software, no per-seat pricing.
- Boards come with documents, a calendar and projects in the same workspace.
- Recurring tasks, sub-tasks and validation person by person; CSV export.

## Where Trello is stronger

- Trello is hosted and zero-setup; Looty needs a server.
- Many "Power-Ups" and integrations that Looty does not have.
- Very familiar to most people, so little onboarding.

## Choose Looty if… / Trello if…

Choose Looty if kanban is only a part of what your team needs and you want to self-host. Choose Trello if you only need simple hosted boards and nothing to maintain.

See also the [FAQ](/docs/faq/) and [hosting Looty](/docs/host/).
