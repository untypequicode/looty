**AppFlowy**: an open-source, privacy-focused alternative to Notion with desktop and mobile apps.

## Where Looty is stronger

- Boards, calendar, mail and Moodle alongside documents, designed for schools and teams.
- A single self-hosted server with sign-in through any OpenID Connect provider.
- Task features such as recurring tasks, groups and announcements with polls.

## Where AppFlowy is stronger

- AppFlowy is closer to a Notion-style knowledge base, with its own AI features and a large community.
- Its apps are cross-platform and polished for notes.
- Looty has no App Store or Play Store apps.

## Choose Looty if… / AppFlowy if…

Choose Looty for team organisation (tasks, calendar, announcements). Choose AppFlowy if notes and a Notion-like workflow are the main need.
