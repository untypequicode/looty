**Vikunja**: an open-source, self-hosted task manager with list, kanban, Gantt and table views.

## Where Looty is stronger

- Collaborative documents, diagrams and notebooks next to your tasks.
- Mail, Moodle and announcements for school or team life.
- Offline Android and desktop apps.

## Where Vikunja is stronger

- Vikunja is smaller and focused on tasks, so lighter to run and simpler to learn.
- It has its own long-standing CalDAV support and a public API.
- If you only need to-do lists, Looty may be more than you need.

## Choose Looty if… / Vikunja if…

Choose Looty for tasks plus documents and calendar in one place. Choose Vikunja for a lightweight, task-only tool.
